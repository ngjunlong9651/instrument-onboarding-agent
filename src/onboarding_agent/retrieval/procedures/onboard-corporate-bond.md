id: onboard-corporate-bond
title: Onboarding a new corporate bond
workflow: instrument_onboarding
steps: lookup_reference_data, check_booking_system, create_instrument
---
Use this procedure to set up a corporate bond or credit instrument (investment grade or high yield issuer debt, Eurobonds with XS ISINs) before trading.

1. Fetch issuer, coupon, maturity, currency and day count (usually 30/360) from reference data.
2. Confirm the bond does not already exist in the booking system.
3. Create the security record. Writes require operations sign off.

Check that the issuer name on the vendor record matches the term sheet. Corporate bonds with call schedules follow the same steps; the call schedule is added manually afterwards.
