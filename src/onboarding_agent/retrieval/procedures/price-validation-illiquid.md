title: Pricing illiquid or untraded bonds
workflow: price_validation
steps: escalate_to_human
---
For bonds with no recent trades, thin quotes or a distressed issuer, vendor prices are not reliable enough for automated validation.

Escalate to the valuation control team, who price the instrument manually using comparable bonds or a model. Do not publish a vendor price automatically for an illiquid bond.
